II
Starting position

Where you are now

The brand is finished and it is good. The wax seal, the script wordmark, and the parchment palette in your existing artwork are a complete, distinctive identity — most of our clients start a project by paying to get to where you already are. Everything in the mockup is drawn from your own files.

What is missing is the transaction. Right now every order is a conversation: a message, a back-and-forth about size and wording, a hand-written price, a payment arranged some other way. That works until it doesn't, and it caps you at the number of conversations you can personally have in a week. It also means the customer who wanted a $54 set of coasters at midnight simply doesn't buy them.

A store does not replace the conversation for complex work. It removes the conversation from the simple work, so your time goes to the jobs that actually need you.

V
How it is built

Next.js, Square, Supabase

Shopify would be faster to stand up and we would normally recommend it. We are not recommending it here, for three reasons that are specific to your business.

The personalization is the product. A live engraving preview that changes with style and placement is not a variant picker, and getting it to behave inside a Shopify theme means fighting the platform on the one feature that differentiates you.

The group stores are custom by nature. Per-organization pages with fixed catalogues and their own branding are an app subscription and a set of compromises on Shopify. They are ordinary work on your own stack.

The fees do not creep. Shopify is a monthly platform fee before you have added anything, and the two features that make this store yours — the personalization engine and the group stores — are exactly the things that become paid apps stacked on top of it. Square takes its per-transaction fee and that is the end of it. On a $148 board the difference is real, and it does not grow as the site does.

One place to manage it — and it is yours. Products, pricing, photography, categories, orders, proofs, and quote requests all live in a single admin built for you to run day to day, without calling us for a price change. We keep the engraving templates themselves — where the text sits on each piece, the safe areas, the font metrics — because those are geometry rather than data, and getting them wrong makes every preview on the site look wrong. Everything else is yours. Nothing about your catalogue depends on the payment processor either — so if you ever change how you take money, the store does not get rebuilt around it.

Practically: Next.js for the site, and Supabase for the catalogue, orders, engraving details, proofs, group stores, and quote requests. Square handles payments and nothing else — Web Payments covers cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash App Pay, and settles straight to your bank. Your Square account is in your name; we never touch the money. Sales tax gets configured against the places you actually owe it, with you, during discovery — not guessed at. Product data, customer list, and content are exportable at any time.

VIII
Dependencies

What we need from you

Every one of these is small on its own. Together they are the only thing that reliably moves a launch date.

PhotographyBiggest risk

Two to three images per product on a consistent background. The Six Eight look you liked is its photography — the design cannot carry it alone. Send us what you have and we will tell you honestly whether it is enough, retouch it for $600, or brief a local shooter.

Product list

Names, prices, sizes, materials, lead times, and what is personalizable. A spreadsheet is perfect; a photo of a notebook page is workable.

Shipping and returns rules

What you charge, where you ship, how fast, and your position on returns of personalized work. We will draft the policy pages; you approve the substance.

A Square account

In Signet Laser's name with your bank details. If you already have one we connect to it; if not, opening it takes about twenty minutes and we will walk you through it. You hold it, you own it, the money goes to you — we connect to it and nothing more.

Domain access

Login to wherever the domain is registered, or the ability to add the DNS records we send you.

Vector artwork

The original wax seal and wordmark files. We have been working from the JPEG; the masters will look better at every size.

One decision-maker

Someone who can look at a design and say yes. Approval by committee is the second most reliable way to move a launch date.
