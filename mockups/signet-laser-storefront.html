The proof process

After you order we lay your engraving out on the actual board dimensions and send you a to-scale image within one business day. Ask for changes as many times as you need — spacing, size, position. The file does not go to the laser until you reply "approved".

Care

Hand wash and towel dry; never let it sit in water and never put it in the dishwasher. Re-oil monthly for the first six months, then whenever the wood looks thirsty. The engraving is cut into the wood, not printed on it, so it will not wear off.

Shipping & returns

Free shipping over $150. Personalized pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind — that is what the proof is for — but anything that arrives damaged or engraved differently than the approved proof is remade at our cost.
